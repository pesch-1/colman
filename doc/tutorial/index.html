Free software for your school library

colman keeps track of books, media and games, of borrowers and lendings – fast with a barcode scanner, for single copies as well as whole class sets. Also suited for private collections.

Download Read the tutorial Source code
Free and open source (GPL 3) · Linux, Windows (preview), Raspberry Pi server, Android app

Features at a glance

Fast with a barcode scanner

Scan the ID card, scan the items, done – lending and returning work without typing.

Class sets in one step

Guided flows for lending, returning, acquisition and liquidation – also for 25 workbooks at once.

Internet lookup

Books, CDs, DVDs, Blu-rays and board games by ISBN, EAN or title: title data and covers are filled in automatically.

Label printing

Barcode labels and spine signatures on label sheets or directly on thermal label printers.

Library ID cards

ID cards with barcode and a freely designable layout; the card can also be used to log in.

Single machine, server, smartphone

One database file on one PC, a server with several workstations (e.g. on a Raspberry Pi), or the Android app with camera barcode scanning.

Your own item types

Add new item types with custom fields (optionally mapped to MARC21) – from books and games to equipment.

Made for schools

Classes and the new school year, reservations, loan periods, fine-grained permissions for staff, teachers and pupils.

Your data stays with you

No cloud: data stays on your computer or your own server, optionally in an encrypted database file.

Get involved

colman is developed in the open, and help is very welcome. The internet lookup has so far been geared mainly towards the German-speaking countries – if you would like to help with catalogues and services for other countries and languages, please get in touch. Further label printer models and translations are welcome, too.

See CONTRIBUTING.md or open an issue.
